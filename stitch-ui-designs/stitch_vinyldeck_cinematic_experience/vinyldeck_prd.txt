# VinylDeck Product Requirements

## Overview
VinylDeck is an immersive, fullscreen vinyl record player experience for Spotify Desktop. It prioritizes emotion, tactile realism, and cinematic atmosphere over utility.

## Key Screens / Themes
1. **Noir Theme**: The core experience in deep cinematic blacks. Features a large centered spinning vinyl, album art label, and brushed metal tonearm.
2. **Glass Theme**: Translucent, frosted Apple-style interface with blurred background orbs.
3. **Aurora Theme**: Midnight cyan and teal colorway with ethereal glow accents.
4. **Vapor Theme**: Synthwave-inspired magenta and purple neon aesthetic.
5. **Paper Theme**: Warm analog cream and bronze tones for a retro-luxe feel.

## Visual Requirements
- 45-60% screen occupied by a realistic spinning vinyl.
- Realistic grooves, reflective sheen, and physical tonearm.
- Glassmorphism UI controls floating below the record.
- Progress ring integrated around the vinyl circumference.
- Elegant, minimal typography.

## Interaction & Motion (Instructional)
- Continuous rotation during playback.
- Subtle vinyl wobble and light reflection shifts.
- Floating background color orbs.
- Spring-easing for UI transitions.
