# vinyldeck_vapor_experience_1

## Visuals (from PNG)

- A complete departure into a retro-futuristic "Vaporwave" aesthetic.
- The color palette is dominated by deep indigo backgrounds, neon purples, and cyan accents.
- A 3D perspective cyber-grid sits at the bottom of the screen, fading off into the distance.
- The typography shifts to `Space Grotesk` (display) and `JetBrains Mono` (labels).
- The transport controls are styled as chunky, tactile hardware buttons with deep bottom borders that visually "press" when active.

## Behavior (from HTML)

- The 3D cyber-grid is created using overlapping linear-gradients transformed with `perspective(500px) rotateX(60deg)`.
- The vinyl record spins at a slower `8s linear` pace, accompanied by neon specular highlights overlaying the grooves.
- A JavaScript micro-interaction is included: a `setInterval` function runs every 150ms to randomize the height of the decorative "LED peak meters" at the bottom of the screen, creating a continuous, lively simulation of audio playback.

## Summary

This prototype successfully establishes a new visual identity (Vapor). It leans heavily into 1980s retro-futurism with neon glows, 3D grids, and hardware-style buttons. The JS-driven peak meters add a lot of dynamic energy to the otherwise static layout.
