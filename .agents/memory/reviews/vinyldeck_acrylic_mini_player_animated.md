# vinyldeck_acrylic_mini_player_animated

## Visuals (from PNG)

- A variation of the acrylic mini-player with a more distinct and vibrant background.
- The background features highly visible, floating orbs of light (cyan and magenta/fuchsia) against a pure dark background.
- The player card itself retains the frosted glass aesthetic, vinyl record, track info, and playback controls from the base mini-player.
- The album art remains a dark, fluid abstraction.

## Behavior (from HTML)

- Enhances the ambient background with three separate floating orbs using independent animations (`float1`, `float2`, `float3`) that translate and scale the blur layers over 20-25 seconds.
- Adds an `animate-shimmerRotate` layer to the vinyl record, which uses a slowly rotating conic-gradient mixed with `screen` blend mode to create an iridescent shimmer effect.
- The play button hover state has been enhanced, scaling up to `scale-110` with an increased glow/shadow effect compared to the base version.
- Retains the slow vinyl rotation, backdrop filters, and groove textures.

## Summary

This version takes the acrylic mini-player and injects more dynamic, floating background light orbs and an added rotating iridescent shimmer on the vinyl record. It emphasizes fluid, continuous motion in both the environment and the player components.
